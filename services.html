Services

Everything in-house, from first survey to final sign-off.

We take on a limited number of projects each year so every client gets hands-on attention from the director. Below is the full scope of work we handle — if it isn't listed, ask us.

All services

Extensions

Single- and double-storey, wrap-arounds, side returns and orangeries. We handle planning drawings, structural calcs and Building Control end-to-end.

  • Planning & permitted development advice
  • Steel frames & structural openings
  • Underfloor heating & bifolds

Loft Conversions

Dormer, L-shape, hip-to-gable and Velux-only conversions. Fire regs, insulation and staircases designed for the space.

  • Party wall agreements sorted
  • Dust-sealed working from day one
  • En-suites & built-in storage

Renovations

Whole-house refurbs, period restoration and structural opening-outs. We respect original features and work around what's worth keeping.

  • Damp & timber treatment
  • Sash window restoration
  • Full rewires & replumbs

New Builds

Bespoke single-plot dwellings and garden annexes. Groundwork, super-structure, M&E and finishes under one roof.

  • NHBC & building warranty support
  • SAP assessments & EPC
  • Air-source heat pump ready

Kitchens & Bathrooms

Full room installations, from strip-out to commissioning. We coordinate suppliers so you don't have to chase three different trades.

  • Howdens, Wren, Magnet & bespoke
  • Wet rooms & walk-in showers
  • Tiling & stone worktops

Groundwork & Landscaping

Foundations, drainage, retaining walls, patios and resin-bound driveways. The unglamorous bits, done properly.

  • Soakaways & SuDS compliance
  • Porcelain & Indian stone patios
  • Garden walls & fencing

How We Work

A process you can actually follow.

01

Survey

Free on-site visit, typically 45 minutes. We discuss the brief, measure up and talk honestly about scope and budget.

02

Quote

Fixed-price, itemised quote within five working days. You see what each line costs and why.

03

Build

Our team on-site, weekly WhatsApp updates with photos, agreed payment schedule tied to milestones.

04

Handover

Snag walk-through, certificates, and a 12-month workmanship guarantee. We answer the phone afterwards too.
