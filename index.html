What We Do

A small team, end-to-end delivery.

Four craftsmen. One project manager. No subcontractor shuffle — the team that quotes your job is the team that builds it.

Extensions

Single & double-storey, wrap-arounds and side returns. From brief to planning, structure to finish.

Loft Conversions

Dormer, L-shape and hip-to-gable. Calm, dust-sealed builds that add a proper room — not a compromise.

Renovations

Whole-house refurbs, period restoration, structural openings. We respect the old and build the new cleanly.

New Builds

Bespoke single dwellings, garden annexes and small developments. From groundwork to handover.

Kitchens & Bathrooms

Full room installs with trusted trades — tiling, plumbing, electrics and finishes under one roof.

Groundwork & Driveways

Foundations, drainage, patios and resin drives. The bits you don't see, done properly.
